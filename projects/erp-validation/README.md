# ERP Validation & Planning Automation

**Documented ERP transition support; completion of the migration is not verified.**

## Problem

A system transition can preserve row counts while changing business meaning. Production and financial records must remain interpretable for planning and reporting.

## Contribution

Career documentation records support for ERP migration through operational and financial data validation, process analysis, and reporting. Earlier Buyer/Planner work included SAP/Excel/VBA purchase-order, data-extraction, and planning-report automation used in personal and colleague workflows.

## Approach and domain judgment

The transferable skill is connecting a data exception to its effect on a real planning or reporting decision. Reconciliation needs a defined record population, consistent field meaning, and an accountable reviewer. These are a generalized validation approach, not a claim that every listed control was implemented in the migration.

## Result and limits

ERP support and purchasing automation are documented contributions. Migration go-live, defect counts, financial reconciliation totals, and ownership of the full program are not established by the reviewed evidence. Item-master repository organization alone does not prove a completed governance system.

**Tools:** SAP, SAP Business One, Excel/VBA, SQL/reporting and data-validation experience. Employer mappings, schemas, records, and internal documents are excluded.
