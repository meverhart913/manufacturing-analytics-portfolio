# Project-to-Role Competency Map

This maps demonstrated work to the role families that currently show the best evidence-to-market alignment. It is not a claim of qualification for every posting or a compensation guarantee.

The current market calibration uses three primary lanes rather than treating every adjacent analytics title as equally attractive.

## Priority 1 — Operational Excellence / Manufacturing Transformation

| Best evidence to lead with | Supporting evidence | Material boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Manufacturing process improvement](../projects/process-improvement/README.md) | [Supply-chain resilience](../projects/supply-chain-resilience/README.md), [career progression](career-progression.md), inventory/decision-support work | Regional/multi-site organizational authority is not assumed merely from project leadership |

Strong title families include:

- Process Excellence Manager
- Operational Excellence Manager
- Continuous Improvement Manager / Leader
- Manufacturing Excellence Manager
- Manufacturing Transformation Manager
- Lean / CI leadership roles with substantive analytics
- Operations transformation roles with meaningful manufacturing content

Why this lane is strong: the portfolio contains direct evidence for manufacturing, Lean/Six Sigma, cost/quality/flow/utilization improvement, analytics used for decisions, implementation, and cross-functional influence.

## Priority 2 — Digital Manufacturing / ERP / MES / Manufacturing Systems

| Best evidence to lead with | Supporting evidence | Material boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [ERP validation / planning automation](../projects/erp-validation/README.md), [Manufacturing Price Explorer](../projects/price-explorer/README.md) | [Inventory decision support](../projects/cr4-inventory/README.md), production automation, SQL/Power Query work | Do not claim end-to-end ERP migration ownership, S/4HANA configuration ownership, or formal MES administration/integration ownership unless a source proves it |

Strong title families include:

- ERP Business Process Analyst / Lead
- Manufacturing Systems Analyst / Lead
- Digital Manufacturing Business Analyst / Manager
- Manufacturing Solutions Consultant
- MES Functional Analyst
- Digital Factory Business Process Manager
- Manufacturing Technology / Business Systems roles

Key screening question:

> Is the named platform one tool inside the role, or is deep platform ownership the role itself?

The first case can be a strong fit because manufacturing/process translation is well supported. The second should be treated more selectively when years of direct platform ownership are central.

## Priority 3 — Supply Chain Analytics / Systems / Strategy

| Best evidence to lead with | Supporting evidence | Material boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Supply-chain resilience](../projects/supply-chain-resilience/README.md), [inventory decision support](../projects/cr4-inventory/README.md) | SAP/ERP automation, production planning, process improvement, quantitative foundation | Direct analytics-team management, advanced model deployment, and broad director-level organizational scope are role-dependent gaps |

Strong title families include:

- Supply Chain Analytics Manager
- Supply Chain Systems Manager
- Supply Chain Strategy & Analytics Manager
- Sourcing / Procurement Analytics Manager
- Planning / Inventory Analytics Lead
- Supply Chain Transformation Manager
- Category / Supplier Development roles where analytics and manufacturing domain are meaningful

Manager/lead roles currently map more cleanly than broad director or Decision Science leadership roles. Director opportunities can still be attractive when they emphasize domain transformation and cross-functional implementation rather than years of direct organizational leadership.

## Supporting / selective role families

| Role family | Best evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing / operations analytics | [Process improvement](../projects/process-improvement/README.md), [inventory](../projects/cr4-inventory/README.md) | Prefer roles where domain knowledge matters rather than generic analytics jobs |
| Production / planning analytics | [Inventory](../projects/cr4-inventory/README.md), [supply resilience](../projects/supply-chain-resilience/README.md) | Synthetic capacity model is a demonstration, not a deployed finite scheduler |
| BI / analytics lead | [Inventory](../projects/cr4-inventory/README.md), [Price Explorer](../projects/price-explorer/README.md) | Current analytics-team management and production Power BI ownership are not proven |
| Manufacturing data science | [Quantitative foundation](career-progression.md), routing-data work, Python demonstration | Production ML validation and MLOps evidence remain weak; this is not a primary lane |
| Operations / analytics management | [Career progression](career-progression.md), [implemented improvement](../projects/process-improvement/README.md) | Historical supervision and change leadership do not imply current analytics headcount/budget accountability |

## Current evidence-to-market conclusion

The portfolio should lead with the combination that is hardest to replicate:

> **Manufacturing and operations depth + Lean/process improvement + supply-chain/ERP experience + analytics/automation used to implement measurable operational change.**

Do not reposition this body of work as entry-level/generalist data analytics simply because some target jobs contain analytics in the title.
