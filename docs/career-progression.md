## Decisions grounded in operating experience

- **Inventory:** distinguish a record that exists from material that is eligible and ready.
- **Capacity:** distinguish aggregate load from a feasible schedule.
- **Supply chain:** balance cost, qualification, inventory, and readiness under the actual supply conditions.
- **Lean:** change the process that causes the loss and connect the intervention to an economic or operating result.
- **ERP:** connect a data discrepancy to the transaction or decision it can distort.
- **Automation:** preserve the operator's required outcome and make exceptions visible.
